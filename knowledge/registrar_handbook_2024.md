## Credit load and overloads

A full-time undergraduate load is 12 to 19 credits per semester. Students may register for up to 19 credits without permission. Loads above 19 credits require written permission from the dean.

## Academic standing and probation

Academic standing is based on the cumulative GPA. Students with a cumulative GPA below 2.0 are placed on academic probation and may register for at most 16 credits per semester.

## Repeating a course

Any course may be repeated once, whatever the earlier grade. When a course is repeated, both grades are averaged into the GPA.

## Prerequisites

A prerequisite counts if it is completed with a grade of D or better, or if the student is currently enrolled in it.
